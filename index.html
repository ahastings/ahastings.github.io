Microscope

Follow the declared accounts that connect Solana transactions. Inspect why they contend, explore estimated conflict-free waves, and check the evidence behind the explanation.

A path from capture to claim

  1. 1
    Public fixture

    Start with a saved public block and its recorded provenance.

  2. 2
    Declared contention

    Follow shared accounts and their read/write declarations to the conflicting transaction pairs.

  3. 3
    Estimated execution waves

    Inspect a conflict-free grouping and the witnesses supporting its bounds.

  4. 4
    Evidence you can check

    Export JSON and independently verify conflicts, components, coverage, and bound claims.

Captured devnet examples and synthetic teaching data are labeled separately. The bundled demo works offline after dependency installation.

The microscope describes potential parallelism under declared account locks. Estimated waves do not reconstruct validator execution or measure runtime speedup. Conditional models retain their assumptions, and verification checks the supplied evidence's internal consistency.
